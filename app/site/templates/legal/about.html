Kwik Klin started in Varanasi with a simple question: why is laundry such a hassle?

You drop off your clothes and have no idea when they'll be ready. You call, and you hear "kal aa jayega." Something goes missing, and there's no record of it. We went through all of this ourselves — so we decided to do it differently.

Built from the ground up

Kwik Klin was founded by Suyash Srivastava, a BCA graduate from School of Management Sciences, Varanasi. Coming from a technology background, he saw that the software available for small local businesses simply wasn't built for them — too expensive, too complicated, or missing the things that actually matter.

So he built his own system instead. Every order is tracked from pickup to delivery. You know where your clothes are and when they're coming back. No guesswork, no "maybe."

That same system now runs other businesses too — but it started here, solving our own problem first.

What we do

Regular laundry, dry cleaning, ironing, and careful handling for delicate garments. Free pickup and delivery across Varanasi.
