How long does a laundry order take?

Most orders are delivered within 4 days of pickup. Heavy items such as blankets and some occasion wear can take up to 7 days. You receive the expected delivery date on WhatsApp.

How do I track my order?

Send a message to our WhatsApp number from the number you ordered with. Our assistant shares the live status of your order instantly.

How can I pay?

You can pay through any UPI app using the payment link we send on WhatsApp, or pay at delivery.

Can I open a Kwik Klin franchise in my city?

Yes. Kwik Klin franchises are open across India. Message us on WhatsApp and our team will share investment and territory details for your city.

Can I use the Kwik Klin CRM for my own laundry?

Yes. The Kwik Klin CRM works for laundry and dry cleaning businesses in any city, starts at ₹999 per month plus GST and comes with a 7-day free trial.
