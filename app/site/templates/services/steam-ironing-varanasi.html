Why steam ironing

Steam relaxes fabric fibres, so creases release evenly with less direct heat. The result is a sharper, longer-lasting finish that is gentler on your clothes than a hot dry iron — and no shine marks on dark fabrics.

It is the right choice when your clothes are already washed at home but you want them pressed properly without spending your evening at the ironing board.
