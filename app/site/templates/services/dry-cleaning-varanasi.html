What we dry clean

  • Suits, blazers and coats — shape and lining preserved, pressed to a sharp finish.
  • Silk and Banarasi sarees — gentle solvent care for silk, zari and delicate borders.
  • Sherwanis, lehengas and occasion wear — embroidery and embellishments handled with care.
  • Woollens and jackets — sweaters, shawls and winter jackets cleaned without shrinking.
  • Delicate fabrics — anything labelled "dry clean only".

Why dry cleaning with Kwik Klin

Dry cleaning uses a solvent instead of water, so fabrics like silk, wool and structured tailoring keep their shape, colour and feel. In a city of weddings and festivals like Varanasi, that matters for the outfits you wear most carefully.

  • Every garment is counted, tagged and checked for stains or damage at pickup.
  • Items are sorted by fabric and colour before cleaning.
  • Stains are pre-treated, then garments are cleaned, pressed and packed.
  • You get status updates and your bill on WhatsApp — no calls needed.

How it works

  1. Book on WhatsApp or our website with your locality and preferred time.
  2. Pickup from your doorstep anywhere in Varanasi.
  3. Dry clean — fabric-wise processing, with updates on WhatsApp.
  4. Delivery back to your door, usually within about 4 days. Pay by UPI.
