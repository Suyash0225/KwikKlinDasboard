Two ways to get your laundry done

  • Wash & iron (per piece) — shirts, trousers, kurtas, jeans and school uniforms, washed, ironed and folded. Best when you want every garment pressed.
  • Wash & fold (by kg) — daily wear, t-shirts, towels and bedsheets, washed, dried and neatly folded. The easy, economical option for the weekly family load.

Laundry for every household in Banaras

Whether you are a family managing school uniforms, a BHU student in a hostel or PG, or a working professional without time for washing and ironing, Kwik Klin collects your clothes, cleans them and brings them back — so your laundry is off your list for the week.

  • Clothes are sorted by colour and fabric so whites stay white.
  • Every order is counted and listed on your bill — no missing socks.
  • Regular pickups can be booked in one WhatsApp message.

How it works

  1. Book on WhatsApp or our website.
  2. Pickup from your home, hostel or office in Varanasi.
  3. Wash — washed, dried and ironed or folded, with updates on WhatsApp.
  4. Delivery back to you, usually within about 4 days. Pay by UPI or at delivery.
