What we clean

  • Blankets and quilts (razai) — single and double, cleaned and dried completely.
  • Comforters and duvets — fresh and fluffy again.
  • Curtains — household and office curtains, dust and odour removed.
  • Bedsheets and bed covers — bulk home linen in one pickup.

Why send heavy items to us

Blankets and curtains are too heavy for home machines and take days to dry on a terrace — especially in the humid months. We clean them in the right quantity of water and detergent, dry them fully so no dampness or smell remains, and deliver them folded and ready to store or use.

Many Banaras households send their blankets before winter to air out months of storage, and again at the end of winter before packing them away.

How it works

  1. Book on WhatsApp or our website. Tell us roughly how many pieces.
  2. Pickup from your doorstep in Varanasi.
  3. Clean and dry — thorough cleaning and complete drying.
  4. Delivery usually within about 7 days. Pay by UPI or at delivery.
